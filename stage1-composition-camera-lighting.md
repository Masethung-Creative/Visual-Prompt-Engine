1.1 SPATIAL FRAMING (Complete Taxonomy)
 │
 ├── 1.1.1 The Vertical Distance Scale (Single-Subject Crop)
 │    ├── Environmental : Extreme Wide (EWS), Very Wide (VWS), Wide / Long (WS/LS)
 │    ├── Full & Action : Full Shot (FS), Medium Full / Knee Shot (MFS), Cowboy / American Shot (MWS)
 │    ├── Conversational: Medium Shot (MS), Medium Close-Up (MCU / Bust Shot)
 │    └── Intimate/Macro: Close-Up (CU), Choker (CCU), Extreme Close-Up (ECU), Italian Shot
 │
 ├── 1.1.2 Multi-Subject & Relational Framing (Character Grouping)
 │    ├── Single Shot (1-Shot)      : Isolates one character inside the frame
 │    ├── Two-Shot (2-Shot)         : Open vs. Closed 2-Shot, 50/50 Profile vs. Stacking
 │    ├── Three-Shot (3-Shot)       : Triangle / Pyramid staging
 │    ├── Group / Ensemble / Crowd  : Collective blocking & power hierarchy
 │    └── Dirty vs. Clean Single    : Including/excluding foreground shoulder/head slices (OTS)
 │
 ├── 1.1.3 Editorial & Coverage Framing (Scene Mechanics)
 │    ├── Master Shot               : The unbroken wide shot covering the entire scene's geography
 │    ├── Establishing Shot         : Context-setting exterior/interior frame
 │    ├── Insert Shot (In-Scene)    : Tight framing of a prop/detail *inside* the current action
 │    ├── Cutaway Shot (Off-Axis)   : Framing something *outside* the main action for context/reaction
 │    └── Reaction / Noddy Shot     :Silent facial framing capturing a listener's emotional shift
 │
 ├── 1.1.4 The Anatomical "Cut-Point" Law (Preventing Bad Crops)
 │    ├── BANNED Crop Lines         : Ankles, Knees, Wrist, Elbows, Waistband, Chin/Neck Joint
 │    └── SAFE Crop Lines           : Mid-Calf, Mid-Thigh, Hip/Upper-Waist, Mid-Chest, Collarbone, Forehead
 │
 └── 1.1.5 Container Geometry (Aspect Ratio & Safe Zones)
      ├── Widescreen Scope (2.39:1) : Horizontal spatial dominance, lateral blocking
      ├── Theatrical Flat (1.85:1)  : Balanced vertical/horizontal human stature
      ├── HD / Broadcast (16:9)     : Modern streaming & YouTube standard
      ├── Academy / IMAX (4:3 / 1.43:1): Vertical monumentality, claustrophobic intimacy
      └── Vertical Mobile (9:16)    : Head-to-toe portrait framing, top/bottom UI safe zones
1.1.1 THE VERTICAL DISTANCE SCALE (Single-Subject Anatomical & Spatial Ratio)
 │
 ├── A. Environmental & Geographical Scale (Subject < 20% of Frame Height)
 │    ├── 01. Cosmic / Aerial Vista (AVS)      : Subject < 1% (Invisible / Planetary / City Grid)
 │    ├── 02. Extreme Wide / Extreme Long (EWS): Subject 2%–8% (Tiny figure in vast landscape)
 │    ├── 03. Very Wide / Very Long Shot (VWS) : Subject 10%–25% (Recognizable silhouette in space)
 │    └── 04. Wide / Long Shot (WS / LS)       : Subject 35%–60% (Full body + generous head/foot room)
 │
 ├── B. Full-Body & Kinetic Action Scale (Subject 75%–100% of Frame Height)
 │    ├── 05. Full Shot / Figure Shot (FS)     : 85%–95% (Head near top edge, feet near bottom edge)
 │    ├── 06. 3/4 Medium Full / Calf Shot      : Cuts safely at Mid-Calf / Shin (European 3/4 walk)
 │    ├── 07. Knee Shot (KS)                   : Cuts safely just above or below the Knee
 │    └── 08. Cowboy / American Shot (MWS)     : Cuts safely at Mid-Thigh (Holster / hand-prop line)
 │
 ├── C. Conversational & Torso Scale (Waist to Shoulders)
 │    ├── 09. Hip / Low-Medium Shot (LMS)      : Cuts at Hip Bone / Belt Line (Includes resting hands)
 │    ├── 10. Medium / Waist Shot (MS)         : Cuts at Navel / True Waist (Elbow bend line)
 │    ├── 11. High-Medium / Ribcage Shot (HMS) : Cuts at Lower Ribs / Solar Plexus
 │    └── 12. Medium Close-Up / Bust (MCU)     : Cuts at Mid-Chest / Sternum / Armpit line
 │
 ├── D. Intimate & Facial Scale (Collarbone to Eyes)
 │    ├── 13. Shoulder / Collarbone CU (SCU)   : Cuts at Clavicle / Top of Shoulders (Head + Neck)
 │    ├── 14. Standard Close-Up (CU)           : Cuts below Chin to Crown of Head (Full Face)
 │    ├── 15. Big Close-Up / Tight CU (BCU)    : Crops into Forehead and Chin (Face overflows frame)
 │    ├── 16. Choker Close-Up (CCU)            : Crops below Lips to above Eyebrows
 │    └── 17. Italian Shot / Slit CU (ICU)     : Horizontal strip isolating ONLY Eyes + Nose Bridge
 │
 └── E. Sensory & Sub-Anatomical Scale (Beyond the Face)
      └── 18. Extreme Close-Up / Macro (ECU)   : Single organ/detail (Iris, pores, fingertip, sweat drop)
1.1.2 MULTI-SUBJECT & RELATIONAL FRAMING (Complete Grouping & Ratio Spec)
 │
 ├── A. Single-Subject Relational Isolation (1-Shot Variations)
 │    ├── 01. Clean Single (100:0 Ratio)       : Zero foreground intrusion; total isolation
 │    ├── 02. Dirty Single (85:15 Ratio)       : Soft sliver of partner's cheek/shoulder on frame edge
 │    └── 03. Ghost / Isolated Single in Crowd : 1 sharp subject surrounded by blurred crowd motion
 │
 ├── B. Foreground Body-Anchor Framing (Over-the-[Body Part] Variations)
 │    ├── 04. Standard OTS (70:30 Ratio)       : Over-the-Shoulder + side of head/ear
 │    ├── 05. Over-the-Hip / Holster (OTH)     : Framed past foreground waist/weapon toward opponent
 │    ├── 06. Over-the-Fist / Weapon (OTW)     : Framed past raised foreground knuckle/blade/gun barrel
 │    ├── 07. Over-the-Head / Crown (OTC)      : High-angle looking down past top of foreground skull
 │    └── 08. Under-Arm / Through-Legs Framing : Framed through the gap of foreground limbs/stance
 │
 ├── C. Two-Shot Screen-Weight & Axis Geometries (2-Shot Ratios)
 │    ├── 09. 50/50 Balanced Profile (1:1)     : Nose-to-nose face-off splitting screen dead center
 │    ├── 10. 50/50 Open Frontal (1:1)         : Side-by-side both facing camera (buddy/sitcom/walking)
 │    ├── 11. 60/40 Dominant/Subordinate (3:2) : Power subject owns center-third; listener cramped on edge
 │    ├── 12. 80/20 Extreme Depth Stack (4:1)  : FG subject in tight CU (left); BG subject full-body (right)
 │    ├── 13. Split-Diopter Dual-Focus 2-Shot  : Extreme FG face + Distant BG face both tack-sharp
 │    ├── 14. Back-to-Back / Janus 2-Shot      : Subjects facing opposite screen edges (trust or fallout)
 │    └── 15. Mirror / Reflection 2-Shot       : Subject 1 in physical space; Subject 2 visible in glass/mirror
 │
 ├── D. Three-Shot Geometric Arrays (3-Shot Power Triangles)
 │    ├── 16. Apex-Up Pyramid 3-Shot           : Center leader higher/closer; two flanking subordinates lower
 │    ├── 17. Inverted V / Valley 3-Shot       : Two tall foreground figures boxing in a smaller center figure
 │    ├── 18. 2-Against-1 Standoff (2:1 Ratio) : Two subjects stacked on one side facing a lone figure opposite
 │    └── 19. Echelon / Diagonal Cascade 3-Shot: Three figures staggered along a Z-axis diagonal recession line
 │
 └── E. Four-Shot, Ensemble & Crowd Ratios (4+ Subjects)
      ├── 20. 4-Shot Diamond / Cross Array     : FG, Left, Right, and BG subjects forming a 4-point compass
      ├── 21. Last Supper / Linear Table Array : Horizontal frieze of 4+ characters facing camera plane
      ├── 22. V-Wedge Phalanx (Hero Walk)      : Leader at center front spearhead; squad fanning out behind
      ├── 23. Hero-vs-Mob (1:N Ratio)          : 1 foreground back-silhouette facing an army/wall of enemies
      └── 24. Parting-the-Sea / Corridor Crowd : Two dense crowd walls leaving a central lane for the subject
1.1.3 EDITORIAL & COVERAGE FRAMING (Scene Mechanics & Multi-Shot Continuity)
 │
 ├── A. Scene Orientation & Geography Anchors
 │    ├── 01. Establishing Shot (EST)           : Exterior/macro location, time-of-day, and weather anchor
 │    ├── 02. Master Shot (MST)                 : Unbroken wide coverage of the entire scene's blocking
 │    ├── 03. Mini-Master / Sub-Master          : Secondary master anchoring a new room zone after movement
 │    └── 04. Re-Establishing Shot (REST)       : Mid-scene return to wide after character exits/shifts
 │
 ├── B. The Shot / Reverse-Shot Dialogue Engine (Axis Coverage)
 │    ├── 05. Angle-On / Shot A (Primary)       : Initial framing on Speaker A along the 180° axis
 │    ├── 06. Reverse Angle / Counter-Shot (B)  : Corresponding 180° mirrored framing on Speaker B
 │    ├── 07. Eyeline Match Pair                : Gaze-vector shot paired with target-object shot
 │    └── 08. Cross-Coverage (3+ Axis Grid)     : Multi-way coverage for roundtables/trios (A->B, B->C, C->A)
 │
 ├── C. Detail, Information & B-Roll Mechanics
 │    ├── 09. In-Scene Insert Shot              : Macro crop of a prop/action *already inside* the Master
 │    ├── 10. Off-Axis Cutaway Shot             : Cuts to secondary object/event *outside* the main action
 │    ├── 11. Cut-In (Axial Punch-In)           : Jumps straight in from Master to tight detail on same axis
 │    └── 12. Atmospheric B-Roll / Pillow Shot  : Non-plot environmental mood frame for pacing breath
 │
 ├── D. Emotional & Observational Punctuation
 │    ├── 13. Silent Reaction Shot              : Listener's unspoken facial shift (where comedy/drama lands)
 │    ├── 14. Noddy / Listening Coverage        : Interview/dialogue listener affirmation framing
 │    └── 15. Reveal / Discovery Frame          : Obstruction clears or character enters to expose hidden truth
 │
 ├── E. Point-of-View (POV) & Perspective Mechanics
 │    ├── 16. True Subjective POV (1st-Person)  : Camera is literally the character's eyes (often shows hands)
 │    ├── 17. Semi-Subjective / Over-Shoulder POV: Camera hovers inches beside character's cheek looking out
 │    ├── 18. Voyeur / Surveillance Frame       : Peeking through blinds, keyholes, security HUD, or foliage
 │    └── 19. Trunk / Fridge / Object POV       : Camera placed *inside* a container looking up at character
 │
 └── F. Multi-Frame & Transitional Containers
      ├── 20. Split-Screen (50/50 or Multi-Box) : Simultaneous parallel action inside divided screen ratios
      ├── 21. Picture-in-Picture (PiP / HUD)    : Inset sub-frame overlaid on main frame (helmet cam, call)
      └── 22. Bookend Framing (Opening/Closing) : Identical visual composition opening and closing a sequence
1.1.4 THE ANATOMICAL "CUT-POINT" & PROPORTION LAW
 │
 ├── A. The 14-Zone Vertical Body Map (Standing Bone vs. Joint Rule)
 │    ├── RULE: Crop through the mid-shaft of long bones; NEVER slice hinge/ball joints
 │    ├── Safe Zones (Green)  : Hair-Crown, Mid-Forehead, Collarbone, Sternum, Ribcage, Navel, Mid-Thigh, Mid-Calf
 │    └── Banned Zones (Red)  : Scalp-Tangent, Eyebrow-Edge, Chin-Tangent, Throat/Larynx, Elbows, Wrists, Groin/Crotch, Kneecaps, Ankles, Toes
 │
 ├── B. Cranial & Facial Micro-Crop Ratios (Close-Up Geometry)
 │    ├── 01. The Upper Eye-Line Law (1/3 Ratio) : Dominant eye sits 33.3% down from top frame edge
 │    ├── 02. The Hair-Crown Tangent (Banned)    : Skimming top hair without committing to a forehead cut
 │    ├── 03. The Mid-Forehead Commit (Safe)     : Slicing 50% down the forehead to force focus onto eyes
 │    ├── 04. The Chin-Tip Tangent (Banned)      : Bottom edge grazing the chin bone ("Guillotine Crop")
 │    └── 05. Ear & Nose-Tip Profile Clearance   : Mandatory 5%–10% air buffer past nose tip in profile shots
 │
 ├── C. Lateral (Horizontal) & Extremity Cut-Points
 │    ├── 06. Shoulder-Cap Split                 : Slicing 1/3 into outer deltoid (Safe) vs. grazing shoulder tip (Banned)
 │    ├── 07. Hand & Finger Knuckle Law          : Never crop across knuckles or amputate thumb/pinky tips
 │    └── 08. Seated & Cross-Legged Cut-Points   : Avoiding the horizontal lap/thigh-fold tangent
 │
 ├── D. Classical Body-Head Proportion Ratios (Lens & Style Scaling)
 │    ├── 09. Realistic Cinema (1:7.5 to 1:8)    : Total body height = 7.5 to 8 head-heights
 │    ├── 10. Heroic / Mass-Action (1:8.5 to 1:9): Low-angle wide lens stretches torso/legs to 9 heads tall
 │    └── 11. Stylized 3D / Mascot (1:3 to 1:5)  : Cartoon/chibi proportions shift joint cut-lines vertically
 │
 └── E. Intentional "Psychological Violation" Crops (Breaking the Law on Purpose)
      ├── 12. The "Headless / Faceless" Crop     : Cropping below eyes/nose to show anonymity, power, or trauma
      ├── 13. Edge-Crushed Short-Siding (95:5)   : Nose pressed within 5% of frame border (claustrophobia)
      └── 14. Bottom-Frame Sinking Crop          : Eyes dropped below the 50% centerline (drowning in space)
